Three pillars

Your CRM, built the right way.

Most CRMs are disconnected from your books. Rheotrade CRM is the same platform — your pipeline talks to your accounts natively.

🎯

Dynamic Pipelines

Design your own sales process — no code, no consultant. Create stages, set colours, assign probabilities, and build Kanban boards that match how your team actually sells.

  • Drag-and-drop stage builder
  • Multiple pipelines (Sales, Support, Custom)
  • Custom fields per lead or deal
  • Probability % per stage
  • Team-based deal ownership
📲

Multi-Channel Leads

Leads from WhatsApp, Meta Ads, Google Ads, and your website all flow into one unified inbox — automatically tagged by source, so you know what's converting.

  • WhatsApp Business API (2-way messaging)
  • Meta Lead Ads webhook
  • Google Lead Form extensions
  • Embedded web forms
  • Manual entry with full history
⚡

Won Deal → Sales Order

Mark a deal Won and Rheotrade instantly creates the customer record and a pre-filled Sales Order — no re-keying, no copy-paste, no gap between CRM and ERP.

  • Auto-create or link existing customer
  • Spawn Quotation or Sales Order instantly
  • Carry deal value, currency, salesperson
  • Full traceability: lead → deal → invoice
  • GST / VAT applied from the first line
