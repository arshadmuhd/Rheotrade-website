Platform

Everything your business needs,
built into one platform

No integrations, no juggling apps. Accounts, sales, inventory, tax — all talking to each other, automatically.

📊

Accounts & Ledger

Full double-entry accounting with real-time P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow. Every transaction auto-posts — zero manual entry.

🧾

Sales & Invoicing

Create GST/VAT invoices in seconds. Manage credit limits, aged debtors, and collections from one place. Auto-reconcile payments.

🛒

Purchase & Payables

Purchase orders, GRN, supplier invoices and payments — fully tracked. Three-way matching catches discrepancies before they cost you.

📦

Inventory Management

Batch tracking, expiry dates, multi-warehouse, FIFO/FEFO costing. Built for pharma, food, and trading companies with complex stock needs.

🏛️

GST & VAT Built In

India GST (GSTR-1, 2A, 3B, e-way) and GCC VAT handled natively. File returns directly, reconcile ITC automatically. No add-on needed.

🌐

Multi-Currency & RTL

Transact in INR, AED, SAR, USD and more. Full Arabic RTL interface. Exchange rate management and revaluation built in.

🏢

Multi-Company

Manage multiple companies, branches, or cost centres under one login. Consolidated reports at group level in one click.

⚡

Workflow & Approvals

Custom approval chains for POs, expenses, and payments. Enforce controls without slowing down your team.

🔄

Settlement & Reconciliation

Match bank statements, settle advances against invoices, and close the month cleanly — in minutes, not days.
